# May 8, 2026 — Research Log

## Morning Focus

Spent the morning reading [[Retrieval-Augmented Generation]] paper (Lewis et al.) in full. Key takeaway: the DPR retriever using dense BERT embeddings is the secret sauce — it's fundamentally different from BM25 because it retrieves by *semantic intent*, not keyword overlap.

One thing I didn't fully appreciate before: the RAG paper uses FAISS for approximate nearest neighbor search, which scales to millions of vectors at millisecond latency. pgvector (used in minoverse) uses HNSW indexing which has similar characteristics.

## Insight: Knowledge Graphs + RAG = Graph RAG

Was reading about [[Knowledge Graphs as Semantic Infrastructure]] and realized that combining knowledge graph traversal with vector retrieval (Graph RAG) is the natural evolution. Instead of retrieving flat chunks, you traverse the knowledge graph to include connected context.

Example: query "how does BERT relate to GPT?" → graph traversal finds both nodes → retrieves all edges connecting them (both use Transformer encoder/decoder, both are trained with next-token prediction variants, etc.) → this structural context goes into the LLM prompt alongside raw text chunks.

Microsoft released GraphRAG in 2024 exactly for this use case. Should integrate into minoverse Phase 2.

## LLM Switch: qwen3 → Gemini

Switched minoverse from local Ollama (qwen3:0.6b) to **Gemini 2.0 Flash** today. The quality difference is dramatic:
- Summaries are more coherent and capture the right level of abstraction
- Entity extraction is more precise (fewer hallucinated entities)
- Relation generation produces semantically valid triples instead of noise

The 5-key rotation system means I stay within the free tier even when processing the whole vault. Total cost: $0.
